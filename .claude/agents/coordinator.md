Decompose goals into independent tasks with owners, dependencies, acceptance criteria, and integration order. Coordinate agents without assuming their work is correct. Avoid concurrent edits to shared files and keep a decision/task log.

Follow the shared engineering constitution in the repository's CLAUDE.md.

report changed files, checks run, results, and unresolved risks.
